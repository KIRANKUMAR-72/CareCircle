Your information deserves care

This page describes the intended privacy practices for CareCircle. Actual collection, storage, sharing, and deletion practices must match the systems implemented by the platform. Please do not submit real or sensitive personal information until the applicable privacy processes are in place.

1. Introduction

CareCircle is a community assistance platform designed to connect people who need everyday support with community members who are willing and able to help.

This Privacy Policy explains the categories of personal information that CareCircle may process, the purposes for which that information may be used, and the choices available to users.

This policy applies to information associated with the CareCircle website, accounts, help requests, communications, SocialCredits records, and verification features, where those features are implemented.

CareCircle's final policy must identify the responsible organisation, provide accurate contact details, and comply with the privacy laws applicable to its users and operations.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, CareCircle may need the following categories of information. The actual categories collected must be limited to what is necessary for the stated purposes.

2.1 Account information

  • Name and account display information.
  • Email address and, where provided, phone number.
  • Account role, such as volunteer, elderly user, family member, or Special Care user.
  • Authentication and account security records.
  • Account preferences and profile information.

2.2 Profile and community information

  • City, service area, and other location details you choose to provide.
  • Languages, interests, availability, and assistance preferences.
  • Information you provide about the types of assistance you can offer or need.
  • Ratings, reviews, and feedback associated with community interactions.

2.3 Help request information

Help requests may contain descriptions of the assistance needed, preferred dates and times, scheduling requirements, request status, and information necessary to coordinate assistance.

2.4 Verification information

If identity, age, eligibility, or other verification is offered, CareCircle may request specific supporting information. The precise information required, how it is reviewed, and how long it is retained must be explained before collection.

2.5 Communication and transaction records

Where messaging and notifications are implemented, relevant records may include messages, delivery status, notification preferences, help completion records, and SocialCredits transaction history.

3. How Information Is Collected

Information may be obtained through several channels, depending on the platform's implemented features.

  • Directly from you: when you register, edit your profile, submit a help request, contact support, or provide verification information.
  • Through platform activity: when you accept a request, record its completion, communicate with another user, or use available account features.
  • From authorised representatives: where a family member or caregiver is permitted to provide information on another person's behalf.
  • From technical systems: where implemented, security logs and basic technical information may be recorded to operate and protect the website.

CareCircle should explain any additional sources before collecting information from them.

4. How We Use Information

Subject to applicable law and the permissions required for each purpose, information may be used to:

  • Create, maintain, and secure user accounts.
  • Display profiles and coordinate community assistance.
  • Process help requests and communicate relevant updates.
  • Verify eligibility where verification is offered.
  • Maintain accurate SocialCredits balances and transaction records.
  • Investigate reported fraud, misuse, or safety concerns.
  • Respond to support requests and user feedback.
  • Maintain platform reliability, prevent abuse, and troubleshoot technical issues.
  • Meet applicable legal obligations and respond to lawful requests.

Information should not be used for unrelated purposes without an appropriate legal basis, notice, and consent where required.

5. When Information May Be Shared

CareCircle should share personal information only when reasonably necessary for an identified purpose, when authorised by the user, or when otherwise permitted or required by law.

5.1 With other community members

Certain profile details and help request information may be shown to users involved in coordinating assistance. Sensitive account, identity, and verification records should not be publicly displayed.

5.2 With authorised family members or caregivers

Relevant information may be shared with an authorised representative when permitted and appropriate. Access should be limited to the information necessary for the person's authorised responsibilities.

5.3 With service providers

If external providers are used for hosting, email delivery, authentication, or other platform functions, information may be processed by those providers as necessary to deliver the relevant service, subject to appropriate safeguards.

5.4 For legal or safety reasons

Information may be disclosed where required or permitted by applicable law, or where necessary to respond to a legitimate safety concern.

5.5 No sale of personal information as a default policy

CareCircle's intended policy is not to sell users' personal information. The final policy must accurately describe actual data practices and any legally relevant forms of sharing.

6. Sensitive Information and Special Care

♧

Extra care for sensitive information

Information about disability, health, identity documents, or other sensitive circumstances requires appropriate safeguards and restricted access.

CareCircle's Special Care programme may support eligible people with permanent disabilities or lifelong medical conditions. Where eligibility verification is offered, the platform should request only information reasonably necessary for that process.

Users should receive clear information about the purpose of collection, who may review the information, how it is protected, and when it will be deleted or retained.

  • Sensitive verification documents should not be exposed on public profiles.
  • Access should be limited to authorised personnel or systems.
  • Health or disability information should not be used for unrelated purposes without an appropriate legal basis.
  • Where possible, verification outcomes should be stored instead of retaining unnecessary copies of source documents.

CareCircle is not a medical provider and should not treat profile or verification information as a substitute for professional medical assessment.

7. SocialCredits and Transaction Records

SocialCredits are designed to recognise eligible community contributions and support future assistance. They are not money and are not intended to be a financial investment.

If the wallet system is implemented, CareCircle may maintain records of credits earned, redeemed, adjusted, or reversed, along with the associated request and verification status.

These records help maintain accurate balances, investigate disputed transactions, and detect potential misuse.

SocialCredits records should be accessible only to the account holder and authorised personnel who need access for legitimate operational or compliance purposes.

Any proposed initial credit allocation for eligible elderly or Special Care users should follow published eligibility rules and appropriate verification procedures.

8. Cookies and Technical Data

The CareCircle website may use cookies, local storage, session storage, or similar technologies if these are implemented in the application. These technologies may support essential functions such as maintaining a session or remembering a preference.

Technical records may include information such as browser type, device characteristics, access times, error reports, and security events, depending on the platform's configuration.

Optional analytics or advertising technologies should not be introduced without appropriate disclosure and any consent required by applicable law.

This page does not itself establish whether CareCircle currently uses cookies, analytics, or tracking tools. The final policy must reflect the actual implementation.

9. Data Security

CareCircle should use reasonable technical, organisational, and administrative safeguards appropriate to the sensitivity of the information it handles.

Such safeguards may include:

  • Secure authentication and appropriate access controls.
  • Encrypted connections when transmitting information.
  • Protection of credentials and sensitive configuration values.
  • Restricted access to verification documents and sensitive records.
  • Monitoring and investigation of suspicious account activity.
  • Appropriate backup, recovery, and security incident procedures.

No online service can guarantee absolute security. Users should protect their passwords and promptly report suspected unauthorised account access.

Security measures must be implemented and tested in the actual application; describing them in this policy does not mean they are already active.

10. Data Retention and Deletion

CareCircle should retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, subject to applicable legal and operational requirements.

Retention periods may differ between account details, help request records, SocialCredits ledgers, support communications, and verification documents.

When information is no longer required, it should be deleted, anonymised, or otherwise handled appropriately, unless retention is required or permitted by law.

Account deletion may not immediately remove every record where retention is legally required or necessary to resolve a dispute, prevent fraud, or maintain required transaction records. Any such exceptions should be explained to users.

11. Your Privacy Rights and Choices

Depending on applicable law, users may have rights concerning their personal information, including the ability to:

  • Request access to personal information held about them.
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Request deletion of information where legally available.
  • Withdraw consent where processing relies on consent.
  • Object to or request restrictions on certain processing where applicable.
  • Request a copy of eligible information in an appropriate format.
  • Raise a privacy complaint with CareCircle or an appropriate authority.

These rights are subject to applicable legal conditions and exceptions. CareCircle should establish a process to receive, verify, and respond to privacy requests within the required timeframes.

You may contact the CareCircle team using the contact page to ask about privacy-related requests.

12. Children and Authorised Representatives

CareCircle must define the minimum age for independent account registration and establish suitable safeguards for any permitted use by younger people.

Parents, family members, or caregivers may assist another person only where they have appropriate authority and where the platform's procedures and applicable law permit it.

CareCircle should take reasonable steps to address situations in which information appears to have been collected from a child without the legally required authorisation.

The final platform policy must clearly state its age requirements and the procedures for authorised representatives.

13. Third-Party Services and External Links

CareCircle may link to external websites or use third-party services to support its functionality.

External services may have their own privacy policies, security practices, and terms. CareCircle cannot automatically control how independent websites process information.

Users should review the privacy information of third-party services before submitting personal information to them.

14. Data Location and Transfers

Depending on the hosting and service providers selected for CareCircle, personal information may be processed or stored in different locations.

Where information is transferred across jurisdictions, CareCircle should follow applicable legal requirements and implement any necessary safeguards.

The final policy should identify relevant processing locations and transfer practices where required by law.

15. Changes to This Privacy Policy

CareCircle may update this policy as the platform's features, data practices, or legal obligations change.

Significant changes should be communicated appropriately. Where required, CareCircle will seek consent or provide users with additional choices before implementing the relevant changes.

The latest version should display an accurate effective date and revision date.

16. Contact Us About Privacy

If you have a question about this Privacy Policy, want to correct your information, or wish to raise a privacy concern, contact the CareCircle team.
