SocialCredits integrity principles

Credit records should be created through verified, completed assistance. Every award or redemption should have an auditable ledger entry and backend validation.

  • Never award credits solely because a request was created.
  • Prevent duplicate, unauthorized, or altered transactions.
  • Maintain a transaction history for every credit change.
  • Apply approved eligibility rules for special allocations.
  • Do not represent credits as cash or promise cash conversion.
