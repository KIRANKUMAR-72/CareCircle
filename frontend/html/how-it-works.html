THE SOCIALCREDITS SYSTEM

Your kindness
should not be forgotten.

SocialCredits are community credits that record eligible contributions. They are designed to help connect the assistance someone provides today with support they may need later in life.

Earn through eligible assistance

Credits are awarded after assistance is completed and verified.

Maintain a contribution record

Members can review their credit history and recorded transactions.

Use credits for eligible support

Credit redemption follows the platform's eligibility and allocation rules.

SocialCredits are not cash, cannot be exchanged for money and do not represent a financial investment.

Understand the Credit System

Identity and eligibility checks

GOOD QUESTIONS

Things you might
want to know.

A few important details about the CareCircle concept and how its proposed credit system works.

SocialCredits are non-cash community credits designed to record eligible help and support future access to approved assistance. They are not money, an investment or a cash balance.

The CareCircle concept is designed for contributions to remain recorded over time so they can support members later in life. The final retention and redemption rules must be defined and implemented by the platform.

The proposed system provides an initial SocialCredits allocation for existing elderly members after the required age and identity verification. This is intended to prevent disadvantage for people who did not have an opportunity to contribute earlier.

Eligible Special Care members are not required to earn credits through volunteering. The proposed system includes an initial allocation after verification, with support governed by platform eligibility rules.

The planned safeguards include verification, completed-assistance confirmation, transaction records, role-based permissions and administrator oversight. Credit transfers must be validated on the backend, not trusted to browser-side JavaScript.
