UNDERSTANDING SPECIAL CARE

Because every journey is different

Living with a permanent disability or a lifelong medical condition can make some everyday activities more difficult. The right support can make those activities more manageable while helping people remain connected to their community.

Accessibility

Assistance that takes individual needs, mobility, communication, and accessibility preferences into account.

Dignity

Members are treated as individuals with choices, preferences, independence, and the right to respectful care.

Belonging

A welcoming community that encourages social connection, companionship, and practical help when it is needed.

WHO IS THIS FOR?

Support for people who need a little extra care

The Special Care program is intended for people whose long-term circumstances may make everyday tasks harder to manage independently.

  • People living with permanent disabilities.
  • People with eligible lifelong medical conditions.
  • People who need ongoing assistance with daily activities.
  • Family members or authorized caregivers helping someone access the program.

Eligibility and any initial SocialCredits allocation are subject to CareCircle's verification process and program rules.

SOCIALCREDITS & SPECIAL CARE

Receiving support should never mean proving your worth.

SocialCredits recognize completed community assistance. However, eligible Special Care members are not required to volunteer or earn credits to deserve help.

Under the proposed CareCircle program rules, eligible members may receive an initial SocialCredits allocation after the required verification. The amount, eligibility criteria, and allocation process must be defined by CareCircle's program policy.

GETTING STARTED

Your path to receiving support

A clear process helps CareCircle understand each member's needs and coordinate appropriate community assistance.

01

Create an Account

Register as a member or begin the registration process with assistance from an authorized family member or caregiver.

02

Complete Verification

Follow the program's identity and eligibility verification requirements. Only submit supporting documents through approved, secure channels.

03

Describe Your Needs

Tell CareCircle what help you need, your preferred timing, and any relevant accessibility or communication preferences.

04

Coordinate Assistance

CareCircle can coordinate a suitable volunteer when one is available and the request can be safely fulfilled.

Online registration, verification, credit allocation, and volunteer matching depend on the corresponding CareCircle features being implemented and enabled.

Respect the member's choices

Involve the member in decisions whenever possible, including preferences about who provides assistance.

TRUST MATTERS

Care should always feel respectful and safe

A thoughtful support experience begins with clear boundaries, privacy, consent, and responsible coordination.

Privacy First

Personal and eligibility information should be collected only when necessary and handled through appropriate safeguards.

Consent and Choice

Members should be able to express their preferences, ask questions, and decline an arrangement that makes them uncomfortable.

Clear Boundaries

Volunteers provide agreed community assistance and should not perform tasks requiring professional medical training or authorization.

Emergency Awareness

Community volunteers are not emergency responders. In India, call 112 for urgent emergency assistance.
