IMPORTANT TO KNOW

Understanding SocialCredits

CareCircle credits recognise community participation. They are designed around reciprocity, not money.

01

Earn through verified help

Eligible credits are awarded only after assistance is completed and verified under CareCircle's rules.

02

Credits are not cash

SocialCredits have no cash value, cannot be exchanged for money, and are not a financial account balance.

03

Support eligible community needs

Credits may be redeemed for eligible future assistance according to CareCircle's published policies.

04

Fairness and accessibility

Eligible elderly members and people receiving Special Care may qualify for an initial allocation after the required verification.

ⓘ

Initial allocations, redemptions, verification and transaction records must follow CareCircle's approved policies. This demonstration page does not issue credits.
