1. Acceptance of Terms

These Terms and Conditions govern access to and use of CareCircle, a community assistance platform designed to help people support one another through everyday acts of care.

By creating an account or using the platform, you acknowledge that you have read and understood these terms. If you do not agree with them, you should not use the services.

If you use CareCircle on behalf of another person, such as an elderly family member, you must have the appropriate permission or legal authority to do so.

2. About CareCircle

CareCircle is designed to connect community members who need assistance with people willing and able to provide it. Depending on the features available, users may create help requests, volunteer, communicate with other members, receive notifications, and track SocialCredits.

CareCircle facilitates community connections. Unless explicitly stated in a separate written agreement, it does not guarantee that every request will receive a response or that a particular volunteer will be available.

The platform is not a substitute for emergency services, professional medical treatment, legal advice, or other specialist services.

3. Eligibility and Accounts

Users must provide accurate information when registering and keep their account details reasonably up to date.

  • You must meet the minimum age and other eligibility requirements established for the service.
  • You must use a valid email address and provide additional information when reasonably required for verification.
  • You are responsible for protecting your login credentials and for activity performed through your account, subject to applicable law.
  • You must notify CareCircle if you suspect unauthorised access to your account.
  • You must not create an account using another person's identity without appropriate authorisation.
  • You must not deliberately provide false information to obtain credits, verification, or other benefits.

Family members and authorised caregivers may assist users with account management where permitted by the platform and applicable law.

4. User Responsibilities

CareCircle depends on mutual trust. All members are expected to communicate respectfully and act honestly.

  • Provide truthful details about your needs, availability, and capabilities.
  • Communicate changes or cancellations as early as reasonably possible.
  • Respect the privacy, personal boundaries, dignity, and independence of other members.
  • Follow agreed arrangements and clearly communicate any limitations.
  • Report suspected fraud, harassment, unsafe behaviour, or misuse of the platform.
  • Do not pressure other users into providing assistance or participating in activities.

5. Help Requests and Assistance

Users submitting a help request should describe the assistance required as accurately as possible, including relevant scheduling and accessibility preferences.

Volunteers should accept only requests they reasonably believe they can fulfil safely and responsibly. Accepting a request does not authorise a volunteer to perform activities outside the agreed scope of assistance.

Users must not submit fraudulent, threatening, discriminatory, unlawful, or deliberately misleading requests. CareCircle may review, restrict, or remove requests that violate these terms.

Request availability, matching, cancellation rules, and completion verification may depend on the features enabled by CareCircle at the time.

6. SocialCredits Policy

♡

Credits recognise community contribution

SocialCredits are intended to support reciprocal assistance within the CareCircle community. They are not money.

6.1 Earning credits

Eligible users may earn SocialCredits for assistance that has been completed and verified in accordance with CareCircle's rules. Merely accepting a request or claiming that assistance was provided does not automatically entitle a user to credits.

6.2 Using credits

Subject to platform rules and availability, users may redeem eligible SocialCredits towards approved future assistance. Redemption may depend on the service category, request requirements, and available community support.

6.3 Credits are not cash

  • SocialCredits have no cash value.
  • They cannot be exchanged for money, sold, or used as a financial investment.
  • They are not intended to be a cryptocurrency, bank deposit, or transferable financial asset.
  • They do not guarantee that a particular service will be available.

6.4 Initial allocations and special circumstances

CareCircle may introduce an initial SocialCredits allocation for eligible elderly users who meet the platform's verification requirements. Eligible Special Care users may also receive an initial allocation under a separate assistance policy.

The eligibility criteria, allocation amounts, verification requirements, and implementation dates must be published by CareCircle before such allocations are offered.

6.5 Credit records and misuse

Credit transactions should be recorded in an account ledger. CareCircle may investigate suspicious transactions, correct verified errors, and reverse credits obtained through fraud or a system mistake, with appropriate notice and a review process.

Users must not manipulate request completion, create false accounts, collude to generate credits, or misrepresent assistance provided.

7. Special Care

CareCircle aims to make community assistance accessible to people who may need additional support, including eligible people living with permanent disabilities or lifelong medical conditions.

Special Care eligibility may require verification through a process designed to protect sensitive information. Users should submit only information requested through an approved verification process.

Eligible Special Care users are not required to volunteer in exchange for assistance under the proposed Special Care policy. Access to services remains subject to published eligibility criteria, available community resources, and applicable law.

CareCircle does not provide a diagnosis or replace qualified medical, nursing, personal care, or emergency services.

8. Safety and Community Conduct

Users should take reasonable precautions when arranging or receiving in-person assistance. Share only the personal information necessary for the arrangement and consider meeting in a suitable, safe setting when appropriate.

Users must not:

  • Harass, threaten, exploit, or discriminate against another person.
  • Request or provide unlawful services.
  • Use CareCircle to solicit money outside authorised platform arrangements.
  • Misuse personal information or share another user's private details without permission.
  • Claim professional qualifications they do not possess.
  • Perform medical procedures or other specialist activities without the necessary qualifications and authorisation.

If someone is in immediate danger or needs urgent medical attention, contact the appropriate local emergency service. Do not rely on CareCircle as an emergency response system.

Users may report safety concerns through the platform's designated reporting or contact channels. CareCircle may review reports and take appropriate action, subject to its capabilities and applicable law.

9. Privacy and Personal Information

CareCircle may need to process account details, contact information, help requests, transaction records, and verification information to operate its services.

Personal information should be collected and used only for defined purposes and handled in accordance with the applicable Privacy Policy and law. Sensitive health or disability information should receive additional protection.

Users should not share passwords, one-time verification codes, financial credentials, or unnecessary sensitive information through public profiles or community messages.

Please read the CareCircle Privacy Policy for information about data collection, use, retention, security, and applicable user rights.

Implementation note: A complete Privacy Policy and operational data-protection practices must be in place before collecting real users' personal or verification information.
