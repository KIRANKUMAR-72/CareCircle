CARE WITH CONFIDENCE

Safety and privacy reminders

A few important practices when arranging community assistance.

01

Respect personal choices

Include the person receiving support in decisions whenever possible. Respect their consent and dignity.

02

Protect private information

Share only necessary details. Never send passwords, banking PINs, or unnecessary identity information through messages.

03

Confirm assistance arrangements

Agree on the task, timing, and communication expectations before assistance takes place.
