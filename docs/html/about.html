OUR MISSION

A DIFFERENT WAY TO RECOGNIZE HELP

Introducing SocialCredits.

CareCircle uses the concept of SocialCredits to recognize eligible community assistance. When a volunteer completes and verifies an eligible task, the system can record the contribution and award credits according to the program rules.

These credits are designed to represent community contributions, not money. They are not intended to be exchanged for cash.

OUR APPROACH

Simple assistance. Thoughtful coordination.

CareCircle is intended to make community support easier to organize, while recognizing that trust and safety matter just as much as convenience.

01

Understand the need

A member describes the assistance they need, including timing and relevant preferences.

02

Coordinate a suitable volunteer

An eligible request can be considered for matching with an available volunteer who can undertake the task.

03

Complete and verify the task

Eligible assistance is marked completed and verified before any related SocialCredits are awarded.

04

Keep the community connected

Request tracking, records, feedback, and notifications can help members stay informed as the platform develops.

RESPONSIBILITY FIRST

Building trust through thoughtful design

Community assistance involves real people and real needs. Our platform should make expectations clear and treat members' information and safety responsibly.

Privacy Matters

Personal information should be collected carefully, used only for appropriate purposes, and protected through responsible security practices.

Clear Records

Assistance status and SocialCredits transactions should be recorded transparently, with appropriate verification and accountability.

Respectful Interactions

Members should be treated with dignity, and assistance should be coordinated with consent, appropriate boundaries, and mutual respect.

CareCircle coordinates community assistance. It does not replace professional healthcare, emergency services, or other qualified services. Registration, verification, matching, and payment-free credit features should not be considered operational until the relevant functionality has been implemented and tested.
