Simple and transparent

From a request to a helping hand

The intended process helps members understand what happens after they request assistance.

01

Describe your need

Select a service and provide the relevant details, preferred time, and location information.

02

Find a suitable match

Connect with an available participating member whose skills and availability fit the request.

03

Coordinate the help

Confirm the arrangement and communicate any relevant details before the activity.

04

Confirm completion

Record the completed assistance for review. Eligible SocialCredits are awarded only after verification under the platform's rules.
