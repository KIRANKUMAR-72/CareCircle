THE HEART OF CARECIRCLE

Kindness today.
Security for tomorrow.

SocialCredits represent the help you give to your community. Instead of letting acts of kindness disappear, CareCircle creates a record of your contribution that can support you later in life.

Earn through verified help

Contributions are recorded after eligible assistance is completed and verified.

Keep your contribution history

Your account can retain a record of earned credits and eligible allocations.

Receive help when you need it

Eligible members can redeem credits for approved community assistance.

Understand SocialCredits

SocialCredits are community recognition credits, not cash or a financial investment.

MORE THAN A HELPING HAND

Care that connects generations.

Today, a young volunteer may help an older neighbour. Years later, that same volunteer may need support. CareCircle is designed around the belief that community care should continue across generations.

We want to create a culture where helping others is not forgotten, and where age, disability or difficult circumstances do not automatically mean facing life's challenges alone.

Every generation matters

Young members, older adults and families can contribute to community wellbeing.

Kindness continues

Contributions today help build a community that can support members tomorrow.

CARE WITHOUT BARRIERS

Everyone deserves
a circle of care.

Some people may be unable to earn SocialCredits because of permanent disabilities or lifelong medical conditions. CareCircle plans a Special Care pathway to help eligible people access community support.

  • Support for eligible people with lifelong care needs
  • Verification to protect fairness and prevent abuse
  • A proposed initial credit allocation for eligible members
Explore Special Care

Eligibility, verification and credit allocation will be governed by CareCircle's platform rules.

BUILT ON RESPONSIBILITY

A reliable support community needs clear rules, responsible members and transparent records.

Member Verification

Planned identity and eligibility checks help build a more trustworthy community.

Verified Assistance

Credit awards should follow eligible, completed and verified assistance.

Transparent Records

Credit histories and transaction records help members understand their contributions.

Community Accountability

Feedback, reporting and administrator oversight help discourage misuse.
